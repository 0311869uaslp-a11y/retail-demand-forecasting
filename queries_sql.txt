Consultas:

¿Qué tiendas generan mayor volumen de ventas?

SELECT
    store,
    SUM(sales) AS total_sales,
    ROUND(AVG(sales), 2) AS avg_daily_sales
FROM sales
GROUP BY store
ORDER BY total_sales DESC;

Lo mismo por producto:
What are our highest-selling products?

SELECT
    item,
    SUM(sales) AS total_sales,
    ROUND(AVG(sales), 2) AS avg_sales
FROM sales
GROUP BY item
ORDER BY total_sales DESC
LIMIT 10;

Ventas anuales

SELECT
    EXTRACT(YEAR FROM date) AS year,
    SUM(sales) AS total_sales
FROM sales
GROUP BY EXTRACT(YEAR FROM date)
ORDER BY year;
